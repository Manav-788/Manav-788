## ✦ About Me

I'm a **Game & VR Developer** and **Computer Engineering** graduate passionate about building interactive experiences, immersive virtual worlds, and data-driven simulations. Whether it's scripting complex physics in C#, crafting 3D assets in Blender, or wiring up a VR headset to a live backend pipeline, I love bringing ideas to life.

- 💻 **Systems & Telemetry:** Writing clean, modular **C#** architectures, event trackers, and JSON telemetry pipelines.
- 🎨 **3D & UI/UX:** Modeling and rigging in **Blender**.
- 🌐 **Connected Worlds:** Building full-stack backends and dashboards using **Firebase** to power real-time apps and VR analytics.
